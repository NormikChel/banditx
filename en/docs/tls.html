banditx handles two scenarios: bring your own certificates, or let Let's Encrypt issue them automatically. Both can coexist: ACME for one domain, a wildcard for another.

#Static certificates

ℹCert chain order

cert_file must contain the chain: leaf first, then intermediates. That's the standard, but people sometimes mix it up with cat server.crt ca.crt.

#ACME / Let's Encrypt

The built-in ACME client speaks HTTP-01 and TLS-ALPN-01. No certbot, no cron jobs. Certs renew automatically 30 days before expiry.

⚠Use staging for tests

In development, use https://acme-staging-v02.api.letsencrypt.org/directory. LE has strict rate limits and hitting them is easy.

#SNI and multiple domains

SNI routing serves different certificates to different hosts on one listener. ACME can handle multiple — the proxy picks the right cert.

#mTLS (client certificates)

For internal APIs, mTLS is a clean way to lock everything down. banditx verifies the chain and (optionally) the CN.

#HSTS and security headers

✕preload is hard to undo

If you submit to hstspreload.org, removal can take months. Test with max-age=300 first.

#OCSP Stapling

Enabled by default for static certs. Also on for ACME, no config needed.
